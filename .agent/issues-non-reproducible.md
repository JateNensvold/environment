# Non-Reproducible Agent Issues

This archive retains findings removed from `.agent/issues.md` after a reasonable
reproduction attempt did not confirm the reported behavior. Keep the original
issue identifier, occurrence count, attempted reproduction date and method, and
any conditions that may be needed to investigate a recurrence.

If a finding recurs, move it back to `.agent/issues.md`, increment its occurrence
count, and add the new evidence rather than creating a duplicate issue.
